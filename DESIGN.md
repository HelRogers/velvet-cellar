# The Velvet Cellar: design system

Status: approved by the host at Checkpoint 2 (24 November 2026). This is the reference for
every section. Do not redesign what is described here without the host asking for it. The values themselves live in `src/styles/tokens.css`.

## The idea

A beautiful speakeasy that happens to conceal a dark secret. The site is the stationery of
a private members' club in 1925: velvet, champagne paper, wax, engraved lettering. The
murder mystery shows only in small, deliberate details (a stamp, a broken seal).

Scene: a guest opens a link on their phone in the evening, expecting a party invitation.
The ground is therefore dark, warm and low-lit, like a room lit by lamps.

## Colour

Sampled from the invitation artwork, then extended with the palette in the brief.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--burgundy` | `#490518` | The ground. Sampled from the invitation. |
| `--burgundy-deep` | `#2c030e` | Shadowed velvet, button text on ivory |
| `--oxblood` | `#6e1225` | Panels, linings, wax |
| `--ink` | `#16080d` | Almost-black, tinted burgundy. Depth and contrast. |
| `--ivory` | `#f6ecd6` | Lettering and body text on dark grounds |
| `--parchment` | `#e6d6b8` | Documents, notices, practical information |
| `--gold` / `--gold-bright` | `#b89b62` / `#dcc48f` | Rules, borders, small labels |
| `--bronze` | `#82673e` | Shadowed metal, rules on parchment |
| `--blood` | `#7a0c17` | Stamps and rare accents only |

Burgundy, ivory and gold dominate. Blood red is used sparingly. Text is never grey: on
burgundy, secondary text is `--text-soft`, tinted from ivory.

## Lettering

All fonts are free, open-licence faces (SIL Open Font Licence, and Apache 2.0 for Special Elite) and are hosted with the site. Nothing loads from a font service.

| Role | Face | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | **Smythe**, set in tracked capitals, with **Elsie Swash Caps** initials on each principal word | Titles, section headings, buttons, invitation text. Mirrors the structure of the invitation's own lettering: plain condensed capitals with a decorated initial. Use the `Display` component. |
| Text | **EB Garamond** (variable) | Paragraphs, instructions, practical information. Italic for asides and stage directions. |
| Typewriter | **Special Elite** | Stamps, file numbers, notices, detective notes. Short labels only. |

Display lettering is never used for long passages. Body text is at least 19px.

## Materials

Textures are generated by `tools/make_textures.py` from filtered noise: no photographs and
no generated imagery. They tile seamlessly and total under 100 KB.

- **Velvet** (`velvet.webp`): the ground of immersive scenes, under a warm pool of light and a dark vignette.
- **Champagne stock** (`paper-champagne.webp`): the envelope, and any gold paper.
- **Parchment** (`parchment.webp`): documents and notices, with burgundy ink.
- **Wax**: the VC seal, an SVG symbol defined in `WaxSealDefs.astro`.
- **Grain** (`grain.png`): a mask that wears the edges of rubber-stamp ink.

## Ornament

- A dotted gold rule under section headings, after the invitation's dotted border.
- The `Rule` divider: a line with diamond ends and a small star, after the rules around the date on the invitation.
- Fan corners on a hairline frame, on screens with room for them.
- Documents carry a double hairline border and, where it means something, one stamp.

Ornament is placed, not scattered. One flourish per composition is usually enough.

## Components

- **Buttons** (`.btn`): square engraved plaques with a hairline inner rule. `--primary` is ivory with burgundy text; plain is outlined in gold; `--quiet` is an italic text link. Never rounded pills.
- **Document** (`.document`): parchment sheet for practical information.
- **Stamp** (`.stamp`): typewriter text in a blood-red box, slightly rotated, worn with the grain mask.

## Motion

- One authored moment: the opening of the envelope (about six seconds, and skippable at any point).
- Everything else is quick feedback: 140 to 260ms, ease-out. Buttons press to 97%.
- The card turns in 950ms with a slight lift.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no flight and no spin. The envelope cross-fades to the card, the card cross-fades between faces, and the idle ring on the seal is off.
- No scroll hijacking, no entrance animation repeated on every section.

## Accessibility baseline

- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 (measured: ivory on burgundy 13.6:1, gold on burgundy 6.0:1, ink on parchment 12.7:1, the lowest pairing in use 5.4:1).
- Every control is a real button or link, reachable by keyboard, with a visible gold focus ring.
- Touch targets are at least 44px tall.
- Both invitation faces carry full alt text; the same details will also appear as selectable text in The Evening's Particulars.

## The sections (Checkpoint 3, awaiting approval)

Each section has its own composition and material, so the page reads as a sequence of
objects rather than a column of cards.

| Section | Composition | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | Centred title under a gold sunburst, drop cap, a closing line in large italic | Deep burgundy |
| The Evening's Particulars | Parchment document with the RSVP button on its lower edge; reservations desk beside it | Parchment on ink |
| RSVP | A reservation slip that opens over the page | Parchment |
| Countdown | An engraved plate with four windows | Champagne stock on velvet |
| An Evening of Intrigue | Six steps pinned along a red thread, alternating sides on wide screens; house rules as a typed note | Ink, parchment note |
| The Confidential Archives | A drawer of tabbed files with a search | Manila on burgundy |
| Dossier | An opened file: clipped portrait, typed facts, stamp, fingerprint, sealed section | Parchment, oxblood |
| Dress for the Decade | Three magazine pages, each arranged differently | Parchment |
| The Velvet Cellar Radio | Two record sleeves with their discs | Champagne stock, black |
| The Cellar Noticeboard | Notes and telegrams pinned to framed baize | Velvet, parchment |
| A Few Matters of Business | A ruled ledger of questions | Burgundy |
| The Final Verdict, A Night to Remember | An evidence envelope and an empty album, both sealed | Velvet |

Murder-mystery details are deliberately few: the broken seal, the red thread, the
fingerprint and redactions in a dossier, the Sealed and Confidential stamps, the evidence
envelope.

Anything the host has still to supply is marked the same way everywhere: a dashed outline
with typewriter text saying what is awaited.
